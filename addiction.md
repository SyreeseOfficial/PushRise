# UX Psychology — Making Apps Sticky

> Source: [The UX Psychology Behind Apps People Can't Stop Using](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2TlIg3VokY8)

---

## The 6 Principles

**1. Smart Defaults**
Pre-fill common choices. Don't make users think. Most people accept your recommendation — use that.

**2. Goal Gradient Effect**
Never start users at 0%. Give them a small head start (show 20% completion immediately) so they feel momentum toward a finish line before they've done anything.

**3. Reciprocity**
Give value *before* asking for a signup. Users feel an unconscious obligation to give back once you've given first.

**4. IKEA Effect**
Let users build or customize something before they sign up (profile, settings, rewards list). They value what they've put work into — even if the work was trivial.

**5. Loss Aversion**
People hate losing more than they like gaining. Frame CTAs as losses: "You'll lose your progress" beats "Save your progress."

**6. Contrast Effect**
Show a high-priced option first so the real price looks like a deal. Anchoring shapes how users perceive value.

---

## Takeaway

Users aren't rational. Design for instinct: make things feel easy, give users a head start, and let them feel ownership before they commit.
