## In-App Terms Block (goes below paywall buttons)

PushRise Pro is available as a monthly subscription ($2.99/month) or a one-time lifetime purchase ($14.99). A 7-day free trial is included with the monthly plan — you will not be charged until the trial ends. Subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel anytime in Google Play → Subscriptions. Payment is charged to your Google Play account at confirmation of purchase.

---

## Shorter Version (if space is tight)

7-day free trial, then $2.99/month or $14.99 lifetime. Auto-renews monthly. Cancel anytime in Google Play → Subscriptions.
