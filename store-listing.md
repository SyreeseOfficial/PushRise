## Full Description

You already know you should wake up earlier. You already know you should work out more. PushRise forces both to happen at the same time.

Set an alarm. When it fires, you do push-ups to silence it. No push-ups, no silence.

**How it works**

Set your alarm like normal. Choose how many push-ups you want to do — 5, 10, 20, whatever your level is. When the alarm fires, hold your phone up and start repping. The camera counts automatically using pose detection. Hit your number and the alarm stops.

That's it.

**Why it works**

Snooze buttons are easy. Push-ups aren't. The second you drop down and start moving, your body wakes up — for real. Not the fake awake where you lie in bed staring at your ceiling. Real awake. By the time you finish your reps, you don't want to go back to sleep.

And because every rep is tracked, you start building a streak. You start banking reps. You start caring about the number.

**Features**

- Push-up detection via camera (pose AI, works offline, no internet needed)
- Manual tap-to-count fallback if you prefer
- One-time and recurring alarms
- Rep bank — every push-up you do goes into a bank you can redeem for rewards you set yourself (TV time, food, money — your call)
- Streak tracking
- Daily goals
- Ghost mode — race against yesterday's you
- Works through Do Not Disturb
- No account required

**Who it's for**

If you've tried every morning routine and quit within a week, this is for you. The alarm doesn't care about your motivation. It just waits.
