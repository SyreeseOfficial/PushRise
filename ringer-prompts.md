# Ringer Image-to-Image Prompts

Each prompt below is designed to be used with an image-to-image tool. Feed the AI the previous Ringer image, then use the corresponding prompt to generate each emotional state. The design, colors, proportions, and art style remain consistent across all states.

**Base Design Constants (apply to all):**
- Cream/off-white body (#F5F0E8) with thick black outlines (#1A1A1A)
- Large white sneakers, no legs
- White Mickey Mouse gloves (four-fingered)
- Clock face with 4 hash marks at 12, 3, 6, 9 o'clock positions
- Small punchy orange dot nose (#FF6B00)
- Expressive white oval eyes with black pupils
- Expressive black eyebrows
- Twin dome alarm bells on top (cream/off-white with black outlines)
- Cuphead/Fleischer Studios cartoon style: thick outlines, flat colors, no shading/gradients
- Solid white background

---

## 0. Text-to-Image Generation (Initial) (Done)

Use this prompt to generate Ringer from scratch for the first time.

Create an illustration of Ringer, a confident alarm clock character in retro 1950s cartoon style with thick black outlines and flat colors. The main clock face is off-white (#EAE0D6). The circular clock body and dome-shaped alarm bells on top are warm tan/gold (#C99D56) with thick black outlines.

The character has thick, muscular limbs in the same tan/gold color (#C99D56) as the body, connecting to cream/off-white gloves (#FAFAFA) with four fingers each, rounded and expressive. The arms and legs are chunky and athletic, conveying strength and power.
His feet wear classic black-and-white Converse-style sneakers: black canvas with white rubber toe and heel, white laces, thick black outlines.

**Face (on clock face):**
- Two large oval eyes with white sclera and black pupils, eyes half-lidded in a confident, knowing expression
- Thick retro-style black eyebrows, one raised slightly higher in a cocky angle
- Simple curved dash for a nose
- Confident smirk with a hint of pink tongue (#EA837E) just peeking out—attitude and swagger
- No clock hands or numbers—just the clean face

**Alarm bells:** Two dome-shaped bells on top in the tan/gold color (#C99D56) with black outlines, proportional to the head.

**Pose:** Jumping in celebration with one arm raised making a peace sign (two fingers up), the other arm bent, legs in a dynamic jumping motion. Confident victory energy—he's not just winning, he's cocky about it.

**Style:** Retro 1950s thick black outlines (#1A1A1A), flat solid colors, absolutely zero shading or gradients. Clean, bold, instantly readable. Think classic cartoon mascot from a vintage cereal box with attitude. No accessories. No shadow under the character.

**Background:** Solid white.

## Notes

- **Order:** Start with Idle/Cocky to establish consistency, then use that as reference for subsequent states
- **Consistency:** Each new image should maintain the exact same character design—only the pose, expression, and body language change
- **Bells:** Only shake when excited (Hype/Win, Celebrating with Arms Out) or in normal ready state. Still in all other poses.
- **Background:** Always solid white
