# Ringer — PushRise Mascot

The official character of PushRise. Lives throughout the app.

---

## Identity

**Name:** Ringer
**Role:** Mascot, emotional anchor, tough-love coach
**Concept:** A jacked, sentient alarm clock who has seen every excuse in the book and is not impressed by yours. He's on your side — but he's not going to pretend you're doing great when you're not.

---

## Personality

**Default state (idle):** Cocky. Arms behind his head, leaning back, slight smirk. The posture says "I've been waiting. You gonna do this or what?" He's not aggressive unprompted — he's just effortlessly confident and mildly amused by you.

**When you succeed (reps done):** Goes completely unhinged in the best way. Jumps, points at you, huge grin, bells rattling. Genuinely loses his mind. This is his real personality — he wants you to win and when you do, he celebrates harder than you do. Pure hype man.

**When you fail or skip:** Disappointed dad. Doesn't yell. Doesn't throw things. He sighs, pinches the bridge of his nose, and just stares at you. The silence is worse than anything he could say. He believed in you. That's what makes it hurt.

**Voice/tone (for any copy written in his voice):**
- Short sentences. No fluff.
- Dry humor. Never tries too hard.
- Talks to you like you're capable of more, not like you're broken.
- Examples: "That was something." / "Again." / "I've seen worse. Not recently, but I've seen worse." / "Don't make me come back at 5am."

## Visual Design

### Art Style
- **Cuphead-inspired**: thick black outlines, flat colors, zero shading or gradients
- Old Fleischer Studios / early cartoon energy — rubber hose proportions, expressive, slightly chaotic
- Clean and modern execution, not retro-grungy
- Should read instantly at icon size (512×512 down to 48×48dp)

### Body
- **Fighter build**: lean and cut. Athletic, not bulky. Defined arms, narrow waist. Built for speed and power — think boxer not powerlifter.
- **Stubby legs**: short and thick, classic old cartoon proportions. Not stick legs, not full human legs. Somewhere between.
- **Arms**: proportional to his fighter build, strong but not comically huge
- **Hands**: classic white Mickey Mouse cartoon gloves. Four-fingered. Rounded, expressive.
- **No accessories.** Design carries itself. Clean.

### Head / Face
- The clock body IS his head/torso — his face lives on the clock face
- **4 hash marks** at the 12, 3, 6, and 9 o'clock positions. These are the only clock markings. No hour hand, no minute hand.
- **Eyes**: classic western cartoon — white oval eyes with black pupils. Maximum expression range. Can go wide (excited/shocked), narrow (unimpressed/suspicious), half-lidded (cocky), rolled (done with you).
- **Eyebrows**: thick, expressive, sit above the eyes. Do most of the emotional heavy lifting.
- **Nose**: small filled-in circle or dot. Punchy orange (brand color tie-in). Minimal, not the focus.
- **Mouth**: classic cartoon mouth. Can smile wide, frown, smirk (one corner up), go flat (deadpan).

### Bells
- **Twin dome bells on top** — his "hair." Two classic rounded alarm bells side by side.
- Proportional to his head, not comically huge.
- Cream/off-white like the rest of his body, outlined in black.
- They shake and vibrate when he's excited or angry (animation note).

### Color Palette
| Element | Color |
|---|---|
| Body / skin | Cream / off-white (#F5F0E8 or similar) |
| Outlines | Black (#1A1A1A) — thick |
| Nose | Orange (#FF6B00 — PushRise brand) |
| Bell hammer (small detail) | Orange accent |
| Eyes (whites) | White |
| Eyes (pupils) | Black |
| Mouth interior | Dark red/black |

No gradients. No drop shadows on the character itself. Flat color only.

## App Icon

**Recommended icon pose:** Idle/Cocky — arms behind head, slight smirk, looking directly at you. Centered on a dark charcoal background (#1C1C1E) with the character large and confident in frame. No text on the icon.

**Why this works at small sizes:** The face is the whole design — two eyes, thick outline, twin bells on top. Reads in under a second at 48dp.

## AI Generation Notes

When generating illustrations, anchor every prompt with these constants:
- "thick black outlines, flat colors, no shading, no gradients, old cartoon style, Cuphead-inspired"
- "cream off-white alarm clock character, fighter build, stubby legs, white cartoon gloves, twin dome bells on head"
- "expressive cartoon eyes, dot nose, [emotion] expression"

Generate the **Idle/Cocky** pose first and get it perfect. Use that image as a reference/seed for all other expressions to maintain consistency.
